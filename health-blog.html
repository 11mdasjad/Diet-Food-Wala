Why Healthy Food Matters

A varied plate makes room for vegetables, fruit, whole grains, pulses and lean protein. Choose a mix of foods rather than relying on one “superfood”.

Read the source: World Health Organization

A Fresh Look at Fried Food

Fried foods can contain saturated or trans fats, depending on their ingredients and preparation. Try steamed, grilled or baked options more often and favour unsaturated fats.

Read the source: World Health Organization

Stay Hydrated

Water is a good everyday drink. Your needs vary with activity, weather and health. Keep water accessible and follow your clinician’s advice if you have a fluid restriction.

Read the source: World Health Organization

Build a Balanced Plate

Combine vegetables, a whole grain, and a protein such as pulses, tofu or chicken. Dietary needs differ between people; a registered dietitian can help you plan around yours.
